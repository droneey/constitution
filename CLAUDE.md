@packages/constitution/blocks/core/core.md

This repository is the Constitution itself: its rules, presets and templates live in `packages/constitution/`, beside the tools that hold them in `packages/`. A rule added or moved is placed by the three questions of core's `placement` chapter — its layer, its axis, its chapter. Its wording is judged by what it means, since `blocks:check` catches only a defined term used out of its place: before a change to `blocks/` or `presets/` is handed back, the `rule-placement` agent reviews it — the rules by their layer and axis, the preset settings by the block they need, its table of answers goes into the pull request, and each of its findings is fixed or answered there. A choice about a rule that the history cannot show — the alternatives a later reader could propose again, what was rejected and why — ships with its entry in `DECISIONS.md`; a rule added, moved or reworded by the Constitution's own instructions needs none, its pull request and its Why being the record. `bun run check` passes before the change is handed back.
