@blocks/core/core.md

This repository is the constitution itself. A rule added or moved is placed by the two questions of "Where a rule goes" — its layer, then its axis. Its wording is judged by what it means, since `vocabulary.yaml` catches only words of one meaning: before a change to `blocks/` or `presets/` is handed back, the `rule-placement` agent reviews it — the rules by their layer and axis, the preset settings by the block they need, its table of answers goes into the pull request, and each of its findings is fixed or answered there. A choice between real alternatives about a rule — one a later reader could propose again — ships with its entry in `DECISIONS.md`, which names what was rejected and why; a rule added, moved or reworded by the constitution's own instructions needs none, its pull request and its Why being the record. `bun run check` passes before the change is handed back.
