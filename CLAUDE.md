@blocks/core/core.md

This repository is the constitution itself. A rule added or moved is placed by the two questions of "Where a rule goes" — its layer, then its axis — and the pull request states both answers; its wording is judged by what it means, since `vocabulary.yaml` catches only the words. A decision about a rule — a rule added, removed or moved, or its meaning or level changed — ships with its entry in `DECISIONS.md`; a change that only says how a rule is held, or words it better, does not. `bun run check` passes before the change is handed back.
