@packages/constitution/blocks/core/core.md

This repository is the Constitution itself: its rules, presets and templates live in `packages/constitution/`, beside the tools that hold them in `packages/`. A rule added or moved is placed by the three questions of core's `placement` chapter — its layer, its axis, its chapter. Its wording is judged by what it means, since the check of the blocks, `constitution check`, catches only a defined term used out of its place: before a change to `blocks/` or `presets/` is handed back, the `rule-placement` agent reviews it — the rules by their layer and axis, the preset settings by the block they need, its table of answers goes into the pull request, and each of its findings is fixed or answered there. A choice between real alternatives about a rule — one a later reader could propose again — ships with its entry in `DECISIONS.md`, which names what was rejected and why; a rule added, moved or reworded by the Constitution's own instructions needs none, its pull request and its Why being the record. The one check, `bun run constitution run check` — the Constitution from its sources running every unit's check — passes before the change is handed back.
