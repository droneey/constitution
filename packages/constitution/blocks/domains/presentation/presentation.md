# Presentation

> Screens a person sees and uses, built from declarative components that take their inputs as props: how they are composed and where their state lives, how components are sourced, built and named, the theme modes they are shown in, the words they show, how forms take input, what every person can do whatever their sight, hearing, movement or attention, and how an interface is proven.
