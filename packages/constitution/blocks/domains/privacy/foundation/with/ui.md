# User interface

> Governs the controls of consent on a screen.

## Controls

### consent-changed-from-every-screen · MUST

Where the product asks for consent, the way to change it is reachable from every screen, in no more steps than giving it took.

| Why |
|---|
| withdrawing is then as easy as giving, wherever the person is. |

### refusal-shown-beside-consent · MUST

The control that refuses sits beside the one that accepts, with the same size and the same weight.

| Why |
|---|
| a refusal drawn smaller or fainter than the consent beside it steers the user to accept. |

### blocked-content-loads-on-the-persons-click · SHOULD

Third-party content waiting for consent leaves a placeholder that says what loading it shares and with whom, and loads it on the person's click.

| Why |
|---|
| the person decides at the moment the content matters to them, knowing what it costs. |

### honoured-opt-out-shown · MUST

Where the law of the region the product serves asks it, a person whose opt-out signal was honoured can see that it was.

| Why |
|---|
| a choice the person cannot see honoured is a choice they cannot trust. |
