# Presentation

> Governs how long an absolute session limit lasts where its person works on a screen.

## Sessions

### absolute-session-limit-short-only-where-a-standard-sets-it → time-limit-adjustable · MUST

Every absolute session limit lasts more than twenty hours. Exempt: a shorter limit a standard the product follows requires, such as the re-authentication NIST SP 800-63B-4 asks at AAL3, named with that standard in the accessibility statement as a known gap or as a claim of WCAG 2.2.1's essential exception.

| Why |
|---|
| an absolute limit nobody can extend cuts off a person who works slowly, and WCAG 2.2's Timing Adjustable lets it stand past twenty hours but names no session limit as essential, so a shorter one is declared, never assumed — NIST SP 800-63B-4 asks 24 hours at AAL2 and less only at AAL3. |
