# Messaging

> A program that does work outside a request: it publishes or consumes messages — events, command messages and jobs — through a broker or a queue, or runs jobs on a schedule, with or without a broker. Its scope is what a message carries and its contract, publishing and its order, consuming and acknowledgement, failures, retries and dead letters, and scheduled jobs.
