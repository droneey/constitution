# Jobs

> A program that runs work on a schedule, with or without a broker: how each scheduled job runs once per tick and is keyed by it, what happens to a tick it misses or to one that comes while its last run still works, how a schedule set in local time meets a change of clock, and, on the architecture axis, where its jobs live.
