# Internationalisation

> Governs how a stylesheet follows the direction of the reader's language.

## Locale

### inline-side-written-logically · SHOULD

A margin, padding, border, inset, float, clear or text alignment along the line is written in its logical form — `margin-inline-start`, `inset-inline-end`, `text-align: start` — never as left or right, save for what keeps its direction in every language, such as a media control.

| Why | Tags |
|---|---|
| a right-to-left language then mirrors the layout from the root's `dir` alone, with no second stylesheet. | [ux] |
