# Internationalisation

> Governs the document of a program written for several locales.

## Locale

### document-lang-and-dir-follow-the-locale · MUST

The root's `lang` and `dir` follow the active locale. They follow a change of locale too, with no reload.

| Why |
|---|
| assistive technology pronounces the text by the declared language and the browser lays out the page by the declared direction, so a stale pair reads and draws the new locale wrongly. |
