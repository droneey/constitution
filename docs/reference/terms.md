# Terms

> Every term the blocks define, with the block that owns it, its axis and the sentence that defines it.

A term is defined once, in bold, in the rule or the passport that says what it is; [Block format](block-format.md#terms) gives the grammar. Its owner is the block of that file, and its axis is the file's: a passport and `foundation/` are foundation's, and a seam takes the axis of its folder. The check refuses a term defined twice, a term of the architecture or of the workflow used on another axis, a term used where its owner is out of reach, and a folder or a suffix written in backticks in another spelling.
