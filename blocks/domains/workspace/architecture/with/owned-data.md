# Workspace with owned data

> Governs what products that own their data share.

## Shared

### products-owning-their-data-share-only-contracts · MUST
Products that own their data apart — services deployed on their own — share through `shared/` only contracts and wire shapes, never business rules.

| Why | Tags |
|---|---|
| a business rule two services share ties their releases together, and they become one program deployed in pieces. | [] |
