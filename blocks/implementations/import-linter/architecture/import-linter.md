# import-linter

> The template's contracts hold core's layer matrix by the folders of its anatomy: what `kernel/`, `libs/`, `shared/`, `contracts/` and a feature's `domain/` may not import; features, adapters and entrypoints blind to each other; adapters kept from their callers; `root/` imported only by the entry files, and no entrypoint imported at all. What no wildcard names — a vendor library kept out of the domain, the delivery layer a block adds — the project adds as contracts of its own, or a reviewer checks.
