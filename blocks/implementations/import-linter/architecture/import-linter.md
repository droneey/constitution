# import-linter

> The template's contracts hold core's layer matrix by the folders of its anatomy: what `kernel/`, `libs/`, `shared/`, `contracts/` and a feature's `domain/` may not import; features, adapters, entrypoints and integrations blind to each other; adapters kept from their callers; `root/` imported only by the entry files, and no entrypoint or integration imported at all; `composition/`, and the delivery layer of each block whose section of the template names it, imported by none of the layers below them; reads and writes apart; no layer folder's `__init__.py` imported; and a role folder of the kernel, and a module of each layer from outside it, reached only through their `__init__.py`. Where a package is imported is held outside these contracts, by core's table and the homes the blocks give; a contract names a module from outside, through `include_external_packages`, only to keep a part of the standard library out of the domain. What no wildcard names — the delivery layer of a block with no section of its own — the project adds as contracts of its own, or a reviewer checks.
