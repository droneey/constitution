# TypeScript with i18n

> Formatting of values in a program written for several locales.

### intl-calls-take-a-locale → formatters-take-the-active-locale · MUST
No `toLocaleString`, `toLocaleDateString` or `toLocaleTimeString` is called, and no `Intl` formatter is built, without a locale or with `undefined` in its place.

| Why | Tags |
|---|---|
| these are the calls that fall back to the runtime's locale, and the linter sees them. | [] |
