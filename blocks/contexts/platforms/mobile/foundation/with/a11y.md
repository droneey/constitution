# Mobile with accessibility

> The mechanisms that meet the accessibility outcomes on a phone or tablet.

## accessibility-properties-on-controls · MUST
Every interactive element declares its accessible label, role and state through the system's accessibility properties.
**Why:** the system's screen reader reads only what these properties declare.
**Check:** review
**Tags:** a11y
**Implements:** `every-control-has-an-accessible-name`

## reading-order-follows-layout · MUST
The screen reader's order follows the visual order.
**Why:** a reading order that jumps around the screen makes it impossible to follow.
**Check:** review
**Tags:** a11y
**Implements:** `operable-by-every-input`

## text-follows-system-size · MUST
Text follows the system's text size, and layouts reflow to fit it.
**Why:** people who set a larger text size need it in every app, not only in the system's own.
**Check:** review
**Tags:** a11y
**Implements:** `wcag-aa-conformance`

## system-display-settings-honoured · MUST
The system's reduced-motion and increased-contrast settings are honoured.
**Why:** these settings are the user's request, made once for every app.
**Check:** test
**Tags:** a11y
**Implements:** `reduced-motion-honoured`

## device-checklist-before-shipping · SHOULD
Before a new interactive component ships, a person operates it with each system's screen reader, and with a switch or a keyboard.
**Why:** each system's screen reader behaves differently, and a scan catches none of it.
**Check:** review
**Tags:** a11y
**Implements:** `interactive-checked-by-hand`
