# Browser with internationalisation

> Governs the document of a program written for several locales.

## Locale

### document-language-follows-locale · MUST
The `lang` and `dir` of the document's root are those of the active locale, and change when the locale changes, with no reload.

| Why | Tags |
|---|---|
| assistive technology pronounces the text by the declared language and the browser lays out the page by the declared direction, so a stale pair reads and draws the new locale wrongly. | [a11y, ux] |
